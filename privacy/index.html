2.3 Device Health Data (Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect)

If you choose to connect Rootbramble to Apple Health (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android), we read the following data types from those platforms:

Data Type Purpose
Sleep (hours asleep) Context for your daily wellbeing check-in
Step count Activity context for your wellness plan
Resting heart rate Biometric context for your wellness plan

Important rules governing this data:

  • Health platform data is read locally on your device only — the raw readings are never transmitted to our servers or any third party. (A derived ritual score computed from this data may be sent to Anthropic if you use the AI insight/coach feature — see Section 2.7.)
  • It is not stored in any cloud database, not shared with advertisers, and not used for any advertising or marketing purpose.
  • We do not request write access to Apple Health or Google Health Connect.
  • Connection to Apple Health or Google Health Connect is optional. The app functions fully without it.
  • You may revoke access at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Rootbramble, or Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Rootbramble.

Apple HealthKit: Under Apple's developer policies, data obtained through HealthKit may not be used for advertising, sold to third parties, or disclosed to data brokers. Rootbramble complies with these restrictions in full.

Sleep, steps, and heart rate data obtained from health platforms constitute special category data under GDPR Article 9. We process this data under your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), obtained via the in-app Health Permissions prompt before any data is accessed.

2.4 Barcode Scan Data (Open Food Facts)

When you use the barcode scanner to add a custom supplement, your device sends the scanned barcode number to the Open Food Facts public API (world.openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product information.

  • The barcode number is transmitted transiently to Open Food Facts for product lookup only.
  • The returned product name, brand, Nutri-Score, NOVA group, ingredients text, allergen tags, and additive count are cached locally on your device to avoid repeat network requests.
  • Open Food Facts is an open-source, non-profit product database (openfoodfacts.org). Only the barcode number is sent, together with the technical data every internet request carries (such as your IP address and device/app information), which Open Food Facts handles under its own privacy policy.
  • Rootbramble is not affiliated with Open Food Facts. The Open Food Facts database is provided under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, with individual product records under the Database Contents License (DbCL) 1.0; product images use Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike (CC BY-SA). See openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use.

Rootbramble's own crowdsourced barcode database: If a scanned barcode isn't found in Open Food Facts or our built-in catalog, your device also checks Rootbramble's own barcode database, hosted on our Cloudflare Worker/D1 database, and — if that lookup also fails — automatically submits the barcode number and the product name you typed in so another user's future scan of the same product can resolve it. This submission is tied to a per-install identifier (Section 6.6) and Cloudflare logs the submitting IP address for rate-limiting. We do not currently auto-delete these submissions; you can request deletion at any time (Section 8).

If you sign up for email updates on the Rootbramble website, we collect:

Field Purpose

Subscription and consent status, timestamps Proving consent (double opt-in), managing unsubscribes, and honouring suppression requests

This is a separate mailing list, independent from your in-app account: it is not linked to your health data, wellness plan, or in-app identity, and it is not visible inside the app. No sequence email is sent until you confirm via a link emailed to you (double opt-in). This is separate from the in-app coach (Section 2.7): it does not have access to your account, ritual, or health data, and no account is required to use it. Your IP address is used only to apply a rate limit and is not stored. See Sections 6.6 and 6.7 for Cloudflare and Anthropic as processors of this data.

2.7 AI Wellness Insights and Coach

Current version: your health data stays on your device. The in-app AI insight and coach are switched off in the current version of Rootbramble. Your supplements, check-ins, wellbeing scores, goals and Apple Health / Health Connect readings are not sent to us, Cloudflare or Anthropic; the weekly insight is calculated on your device. The rest of this section describes how the feature works if a future version turns it on, which will again require your separate consent first.

If you view your weekly ritual insight or ask a question through the in-app supplement coach, the following is sent to our backend and forwarded to our AI provider, Anthropic (maker of Claude), to generate a response:

Feature Data sent
Weekly ritual insight Your average ritual score for the week, days completed out of total days, current streak, the week label (e.g. "Sep 15–21"), the names of the supplement you logged most consistently and least consistently (and how many days the latter was missed), your self-reported wellbeing trend (e.g. "energy up 35%"), your wellness goals, and your app language
Ask-the-coach question The question you ask, your wellness goals, the insight text shown on screen at the time (which may include supplement names and pattern data), and your app language

This request is proxied through a Cloudflare Worker we operate — Cloudflare relays it but does not store it (see Section 6.6). Anthropic processes the request to generate the response and does not use API data to train its models. The structured fields listed above do not include your disclosed health conditions, medications, or dietary restrictions (Section 2.2); however, if you type that information into a free-text question to the coach, it is sent as-is and is not filtered out. See Section 6.7 for details on Anthropic as a processor.

Separate consent for this sharing: Sharing your ritual/wellness data with Anthropic requires its own dedicated, revocable consent — distinct from the general health-data consent in Section 2.2 and from analytics consent. You are shown exactly what will be sent and to whom before it happens, and nothing is shared until you agree; you can review or withdraw this consent anytime in Profile → AI Insight & Coach. Without it, the weekly insight still works from an on-device summary that never leaves your phone; the coach's free-text Q&A requires this consent to function at all, since it has no non-AI equivalent. See our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for the Washington My Health My Data Act-specific disclosures governing this sharing.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights:

Art. 15 — Right of access: Request a copy of your personal data.

Art. 16 — Right to rectification: Correct inaccurate data.

Art. 17 — Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Request deletion of your data. Local data: delete within the app (Settings → Delete My Data). Cloud data: submit a request to tekeshine@gmail.com — completed within 30 days.

Art. 18 — Right to restriction: Request that we restrict processing.

Art. 20 — Right to data portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format (CSV export available in Settings).

Art. 21 — Right to object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Art. 22 — Automated decision-making: Your wellness plan is generated algorithmically. You can override plan items manually. No decisions with significant legal effect are made automatically.

To exercise any right, email tekeshine@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not deny you goods or services, charge different prices, or provide a different level of service because you exercised any right under this section.

Authorized Agent: You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. We may require proof of the agent's authorization and verification of your identity.

11. FTC Health Breach Notification Rule

For users in the United States, we comply with the Federal Trade Commission Health Breach Notification Rule. In the event of a security breach involving your health data, we will:

  • Notify you without unreasonable delay, and in no case later than 60 calendar days after discovering the breach — the clock starts the day the breach becomes known to us or reasonably should have, and if we finish our assessment sooner than 60 days, we notify sooner, not on day 60 regardless
  • Notify the FTC: for a breach affecting 500 or more people, at the same time as the individual notices above (also within 60 days); for a breach affecting fewer than 500 people, via the FTC's online Notice of Breach of Health Information form within 60 days of the end of the calendar year in which the breach occurred
  • For breaches affecting 500 or more users in a US state or territory, notify prominent media outlets in those areas

Notifications will be delivered via email (if you have provided one), in-app notification, and push notification.
