3.1 Experiment Mode

Rootbramble's Experiment feature allows you to track your self-reported wellbeing scores (energy, sleep, mood) before and during a period when you are taking a particular supplement. Results are displayed as numeric deltas based solely on your own self-reported data.

Experiment results are personal observations, not clinical evidence. A positive delta (e.g., "Energy ↑0.8") means your self-reported energy scores were higher on average during the tracked period — it does not constitute proof that the supplement caused that change, and it does not constitute a health claim, medical advice, or a recommendation to increase, decrease, or continue any supplement. Many factors affect wellbeing scores. Rootbramble makes no representation about supplement efficacy.

3.2 Supplement Timing Tips

Rootbramble may display timing suggestions for supplements in your plan based on known interactions in wellness literature (e.g., spacing iron and zinc). These are general informational tips only — not personalised medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional if you have questions about supplement interactions with medications or health conditions.

If you share an export with a healthcare professional, clearly communicate that it is self-tracked wellness data from a habit-tracking app, not a clinically validated report.

These features are switched off in the current version of Rootbramble; the weekly insight you see today is calculated on your device without any AI model. This section applies only if a future version turns them on, which will require your separate consent first (see Privacy Policy §2.7).

When enabled, Rootbramble's weekly ritual insight and in-app coach answer your questions using a third-party AI model (Anthropic's Claude). These responses are generated automatically from your ritual data and are not reviewed by a person before you see them. They are general wellness commentary — not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a guarantee of any outcome. Always use your own judgment and consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions.

3.5 Lab Marker Tracking

Rootbramble lets you manually log lab/biomarker values (e.g., vitamin D, ferritin) and displays them alongside a general reference range. This reference range reflects general population data, not a personalized interpretation of your health — it is not a diagnosis, not a clinical reading of your labs, and not a substitute for review by a licensed clinician. Only a healthcare professional with your full medical history can interpret lab results. Always discuss your actual lab results with your doctor.
