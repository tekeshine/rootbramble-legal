Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy — Rootbramble

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This is a standalone policy required under Washington State's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and equivalent consumer health data laws. It applies specifically to Consumer Health Data as defined by that law. It does not contain marketing content and is not combined with our general Privacy Policy, which covers all other categories of data — see PRIVACY_POLICY.md for the full picture of how Rootbramble handles personal data generally.

Lab report scanning. If you use the camera or photo library to scan a lab report instead of typing values in by hand: the image you select and the text recognized from it are processed on-device (Apple/Google's on-device text recognition) — we do not transmit the image or the recognized text anywhere. Recognized values stay in memory for you to review, edit, and select before anything is saved; only the specific marker values you choose to save are written to the app's storage. A camera-taken photo's temporary copy is deleted from the device once recognition finishes; a photo you pick from your library is not touched or deleted, since it's your own photo.

3. Purpose of Collection and Processing

We collect and process consumer health data solely to:

  • Generate and filter your personalised wellness plan, excluding items contraindicated by your disclosed conditions or medications
  • Power your daily check-in, streak, and progress tracking features
  • Generate your weekly ritual insight and answer questions you ask the in-app coach, when you actively use those optional features
  • Respond to a data-subject rights request, or comply with a legal obligation

We do not use consumer health data for advertising, for cross-context behavioural profiling, or to make inferences about you beyond what is directly relevant to the purposes above.

Current version: the in-app AI insight and coach are switched off, so Rootbramble does not share consumer health data with anyone; it stays on your device. The table below applies only if a future version turns the feature on, and only after your separate consent.

Third party What is shared When
Anthropic (AI provider) Ritual score, adherence days, streak, wellness goals, supplement names, self-reported wellbeing trend; and, for the coach feature, the free-text question you type Only when you actively open your weekly insight or ask the in-app coach a question — an optional feature you must choose to use

We do not share consumer health data with data brokers, advertisers, or any party for cross-context behavioural advertising. Device health data (HealthKit/Health Connect) and disclosed health conditions/dietary restrictions are not included in the structured data sent to Anthropic — only the free-text coach question is unfiltered, and only if you choose to type health-related detail into it. Barcode lookups (Open Food Facts) and analytics events (PostHog) do not contain consumer health data as defined by this policy.

5. Consent

We do not collect, process, or share consumer health data without your consent, obtained through an in-app screen before that data is collected, separate from your acceptance of the Terms of Use. You may withdraw consent at any time — see Section 6.

Sharing with the AI insight/coach feature specifically requires a dedicated, revocable opt-in — distinct from the general health-data consent above and from analytics consent, consistent with the higher bar this law sets for consent to sharing as opposed to collection. The first time you would see an AI-generated insight or ask the in-app coach a question, you are shown exactly what is sent and to whom, and nothing is shared until you agree. You can review or withdraw this consent at any time in Profile → AI Insight & Coach. Without this consent, the weekly insight still works using an on-device summary that is never sent anywhere; only the coach's free-text Q&A requires it to function at all.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we are processing your consumer health data
  • Access the consumer health data we hold about you
  • Withdraw consent to its collection or sharing at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal
  • Delete your consumer health data — locally stored data can be deleted directly in the app (Settings → Delete My Data); data held by a processor (see Section 4) can be deleted by request

To exercise any of these rights, email tekeshine@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any right under this policy.

Appeals: If we decline your request in whole or in part, we will explain why in our response. You may appeal that decision by replying to the same email within 45 days of our response. We will respond to your appeal within 45 days of receiving it. If we uphold the original decision, we will explain why and provide tekeshine@gmail.com as a contact for any further complaint you wish to submit to your state attorney general or applicable regulator.

7. No Sale of Consumer Health Data

We do not sell consumer health data, and we will not do so in the future without first obtaining your valid, specific, written authorization as required by law, separate from and in addition to the consent described above.

8. Geofencing

We do not, and will not, implement geofencing (as defined by RCW 19.373) around any healthcare facility for the purpose of identifying, tracking, collecting data from, or sending notifications to consumers regarding their consumer health data or their receipt of health care services.

9. Data Retention

Consumer health data you enter is stored locally on your device, either in our app database or, for lab results and supplement notes, in your device's secure Keychain/Keystore. Database contents are removed automatically when you uninstall the app. Keychain/Keystore-backed data is not automatically removed by uninstalling the app — this is a platform behavior outside our control — but is cleared when you delete your data in-app, or automatically purged the next time Rootbramble is freshly installed on that device. See Section 8 of the general Privacy Policy for full retention details across all data categories.

10. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you of material changes to this policy via in-app notification, and the App will block further use until you affirmatively re-accept the revised policy — you will see this the next time you open the app after the change ships, not on a 30-day delay.
